# Design note
A tapered oval load path carries the wrist fork into the elbow flange, with a straight cable passage.
I tried a split-rail arm; it saved mass but read as two exposed struts, not a continuous form.
A narrow root deflected too much, and a shaft grown from inside the flange still concentrated stress near the support.
I widened the root; that reduced the coarse proxy stresses but created a prominent ledge.
A 1 mm fillet was tried and dropped: it greatly increased local mesh density and did not demonstrate stress relief.
With another hour I would rebuild the elbow junction as a tangent transition, model the real bolt and pin thrust contact, and converge the local stresses.
The least satisfying part is the elbow shoulder: it still looks like a separate collar, and the held boundary dominates the study.
